## Website integration checklist

1. Publish current approved English/Turkish legal documents in Studio or its legal pages; preserve versioned URLs or archives.
2. Show the applicable Terms and Privacy Notice before sign-up or service activation, and capture an affirmative acceptance record (document version, timestamp, user/organization, method).
3. For custom paid offers, disclose price, currency, taxes, billing cycle, auto-renewal, cancellation, and mandatory consumer information before acceptance.
4. Make cancellation and deletion pathways available and document how account deletion and legally required retention are handled.
5. Complete any mandatory legal provider identity/address disclosures and verify applicable cross-border data-transfer and DPA requirements before commercial launch.

Changes here do not automatically deploy to **studio.tryvira.xyz**. The Studio deployment must link to the approved documents and implement its own acceptance and disclosure flow.
