## 2. Registration and Onboarding

Registration may involve name, email address, sign-in credentials, and session identifiers. Onboarding may involve phone number, company/organization, country, position, and intended use of Vira.

| Onboarding and workspace configuration | Setting up the requested workspace, role, and Service | Article 5(2)(c) where necessary; another valid ground for different purposes |
| Security and authorization records | Preventing unauthorized access and securing the Service | Legitimate interests, Article 5(2)(f), subject to protection of fundamental rights, or another valid ground |
| Mandatory commercial records | Compliance with legal requirements | Legal obligation, Article 5(2)(ç) |
| Non-essential advertising or consent-requiring analytics | Advertising and performance measurement | Explicit consent, Article 5(1), where legally required |

When Google or GitHub sign-in is chosen, authorized account identifiers, names, and email addresses may be collected. The fact that company or position information is requested does not mean every such field is mandatory; the actual form and processing purpose determine whether it is required.

Authorized integrations may submit conversations, AI inputs/outputs, agent events, tool parameters, evaluation results, and trace/receipt data.

This data supports requested Observe/Teach functions and AI behavior evaluation. Connection permissions, customer instructions, and applicable processing grounds matter for each integration. ## 4. Collection and Recipients

Information is collected electronically through website/Studio forms, OAuth sign-in, account sessions, authorized API/SDK/MCP connections, uploads, security events, and lawfully permitted analytics or advertising technologies.

Depending on purpose and configuration, recipients may include hosting or storage providers (such as Supabase), Google/GitHub sign-in providers, authorized AI model providers, security/error monitoring services, and analytics or advertising vendors.

Primary customer storage is in the EU, so international transfers or remote access may occur. Transfers subject to KVKK must meet its Article 9 requirements. See the [Provider Register](subprocessors.en.md). A provider being listed does not mean it receives every customer's data.

## 5. Notice Is Different from Consent

Providing a KVKK notice is a transparency obligation to be met **before collecting personal data** where applicable. Reading or accepting the notice is not explicit consent to advertising, analytics, marketing, or any other activity that requires specific consent.

Where consent is required, a separate freely given decision is requested. Cookie choices are offered through **Accept / Reject / Preferences**; see the [Cookie Policy](cookies.en.md).

## 6. Retention and Deletion

Account information and configurations are processed while necessary to provide the requested Service and meet legal obligations. Observe/Teach, conversation, trace, and security data follow category-dependent retention criteria explained in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).

Following account deletion, information subject to deletion is deleted within **30 days**, subject to legally justified exceptions. Eligible Observe and Teach data may be requested for deletion without account closure.

## 7. KVKK Article 11 Rights and Requests

You may ask whether your data is processed, obtain information about purposes and recipients, request correction or lawful deletion/destruction, request notification of corrections/deletion to recipients, object to qualifying exclusively automated decisions, and seek compensation where unlawful processing causes damage.

General inquiries may be sent to **hello@tryvira.xyz**. Formal KVKK applications must follow Article 13 and applicable application rules; reasonable identity and authority verification may be required. Applications are resolved as quickly as possible and within **30 days**. Statutory complaint rights before the Personal Data Protection Board remain available.
