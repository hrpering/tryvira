## 6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU GDPR applies, the legal basis depends on the activity:

| Activity | Relevant GDPR basis |
| --- | --- |
| Account creation, authentication, and delivery of requested Services | Performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b), where applicable |
| Security, fraud prevention, and essential service operations | Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), where justified |
| Compliance, accounting, and lawful requests | Legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c), where applicable |
| Optional advertising and non-essential analytics | Consent, Article 6(1)(a), where required |
| Voluntary marketing communications | Consent or another lawful basis where legally allowed |
| Processing organizational end-user data | The customer's applicable legal basis, instructions, and our processor obligations |

A legal basis is assessed for the actual processing purpose; the table does not mean all activities use every listed basis. Legitimate interests require appropriate balancing against individual rights.

Where Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (**KVKK**) applies, processing relies on the applicable conditions under Articles 5 and 6, and international transfers must also satisfy Article 9. Consent is requested where legally required and can be withdrawn without affecting processing lawfully completed before withdrawal.

Eligible Observe and Teach information can also be requested for deletion without closing the account. Records independently held by third-party providers may be subject to separate arrangements and cannot always be removed simply by deleting a Vira account.
