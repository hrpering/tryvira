## 3. Purposes and Legal Grounds

The actual legal ground depends on the processing activity. Under KVKK Article 5, relevant grounds may include:

| Activity | Possible KVKK legal ground |
| --- | --- |
| Registration, authentication and requested Services | Necessity for establishing or performing a contract, Article 5(2)(c) |
| Service support and account management | Contract performance or a justified legitimate interest, depending on the activity |
| Access security and prevention of abuse | Legitimate interest, Article 5(2)(f), provided fundamental rights are protected |
| Statutory accounting and compliance | Legal obligation, Article 5(2)(ç) |
| Protection of legal claims | Establishment, exercise or protection of a right, Article 5(2)(e) |
| Customer-requested behavior operations, Observe and Teach | Contract necessity, another applicable condition or explicit consent where required |
| Non-essential advertising and consent-requiring analytics | Explicit consent, Article 5(1) |
| Optional promotional communications | The applicable marketing rules and a valid personal-data processing ground |

Not every listed ground applies to every activity. Where special-category data is processed, the conditions of KVKK Article 6 must be assessed separately.

Vira does not use User Content to train general-purpose AI models or for general product/model improvement. Authorized Observe and Teach processing is performed as part of the service requested by the customer.
