## 1. Scope and Operator

This Addendum supplements the [Vira Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md) for residents of US states with applicable consumer privacy laws, including, where relevant, the **California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA)** and other state comprehensive privacy statutes.

Vira is operated by **Esad Türkel**, an independent individual service provider based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Vira is not presently a separately incorporated legal entity.

US state privacy statutes differ in applicability thresholds, exceptions, covered persons, defined roles, and available rights. This Addendum describes how those rights relate to Vira **to the extent a particular law applies**; it does not claim that Vira meets every statutory coverage threshold.

Some information is processed by Vira as a service provider or processor on behalf of a business customer. In that case, the customer's consumer-facing privacy notice and instructions may govern the underlying data.

We process the above information to operate accounts, authenticate users, provide AI-behavior and developer Services, administer Observe and Teach when authorized, maintain security, respond to support requests, manage accepted commercial relationships, and measure website or advertising activity where permitted.

Vira does **not** use customer User Content for general-purpose AI model training or general product/model improvement.

Retention is based on the account/workspace lifecycle, the specific feature, customer instructions where applicable, security and legal needs, and the relevant provider's settings. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md). Information subject to deletion following account closure is deleted within **30 days**, except when lawful retention applies.

## 5. Advertising, Sale, Sharing and Targeted Advertising

Vira uses **Meta Pixel, Microsoft Advertising, Google Analytics 4**, and other disclosed measurement technologies. Subject to configuration, they may receive browser, device, click, event or conversion identifiers.

Under some US state privacy laws, providing information for **cross-context behavioral advertising** may qualify as "sharing," "sale," or "targeted advertising" **even if no money is received**. The classification depends on the actual vendor terms, disclosures, settings, and relevant law. Vira does not represent that all advertising-related data disclosures are categorically outside those definitions.

The ordinary Vira service does not require that a customer's private prompts, conversations, or Behavior Packs be transferred to advertising networks for model training. Website marketing/measurement data is distinct from Customer User Content processed for Observe and Teach.

Where the applicable law grants an opt-out right for sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or specified profiling, that right is available subject to the law's conditions.

## 6. Consumer Privacy Rights

Depending on state law and its coverage, you may have the right to:
1. **Know or access** categories or specific personal information and certain processing and disclosure details.
2. **Correct** inaccurate personal information.
3. **Delete** eligible personal information, subject to statutory exceptions.
4. **Obtain a portable copy** of qualifying information.
5. **Opt out** of qualifying sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling involving significant decisions.
6. **Limit certain uses of sensitive personal information**, where the relevant law provides that right.
7. **Withdraw consent**, where consent is required.
8. **Appeal** a denied privacy request, where state law requires an appeals process.
9. **Receive non-discriminatory treatment** for exercising protected rights.

The scope of each right varies by state and the type of processing. Other legal rights, including under California law, are not limited by this summary.

You may submit a privacy request to **hello@tryvira.xyz** and identify the state of residence and the right you wish to exercise. You may also use available account and cookie controls for access, deletion, or consent-related choices.

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity, residency, account authority, or an authorized agent's capacity, subject to applicable restrictions. We will not request unrelated sensitive information solely to authenticate routine requests.

Vira will respond within legally applicable periods, provide reasons for a denial where required, and explain appeal procedures if the governing state law requires them.

For organizational end-user information that Vira processes solely on a customer's behalf, we may direct the request to that customer and provide assistance as legally required.

## 8. Cookie Choices and Universal Opt-Out Signals

Vira's [Cookie Policy](cookies.en.md) explains **Accept**, **Reject**, and **Preferences** choices for optional website tracking. Users can withdraw or change their selection through the website's available consent controls.

Some state privacy laws require a covered business to recognize qualifying **universal opt-out mechanisms**, such as **Global Privacy Control (GPC)**, as valid choices regarding sale or sharing or targeted advertising. Their legal effect depends on the law and actual data flow.

Where a relevant statutory opt-out right applies, submit the request by using available privacy choices or writing to **hello@tryvira.xyz**. Any technical implementation of universal signals must meet the requirements of the applicable jurisdiction; a cookie consent banner by itself is not a substitute for every required US state opt-out mechanism.

## 9. Sensitive Data and Children

Ordinary Vira account creation does not request Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, precise geolocation, health records, or other information commonly regulated as sensitive. Nonetheless, enterprise customers and users can supply sensitive information in content or connected systems.

If a law requires consent or a right to limit certain processing of sensitive information, the applicable requirements must be met in the relevant context. Customer-controlled sensitive data may be subject to the customer's own instructions and notices.

Vira Services are intended for users **18 and older**. We do not knowingly offer accounts to minors. State-specific protections for children's information remain applicable where required.

## 10. AI, Profiling and Automated Decisions

Vira offers tools to specify, evaluate, and govern AI behavior. Teach recommendations do not automatically make live changes.

Vira's availability as an AI infrastructure provider does not establish that Vira itself makes decisions about a particular individual's employment, credit, housing, health, or other legally significant status. If a business customer uses a connected system for these purposes, relevant profiling and automated-decision rules may apply to that customer's processing.

Where a state law provides an enforceable opt-out, access, or appeal right in relation to qualifying profiling or automated decisionmaking, those statutory rights are not waived by using Vira.
