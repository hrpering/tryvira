## 1. Scope and Legal Framework

This Addendum supplements the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md) and, where relevant, the [Data Processing Addendum](dpa.en.md) for personal data processing subject to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the **General Data Protection Regulation**, or "GDPR").

Vira is operated by **Esad Türkel**, an independent individual operator established in Istanbul, Türkiye. Vira is not currently a separately incorporated legal entity.

GDPR applicability depends on the actual activity, including whether services are offered to individuals in the EEA or behavior of individuals in the EEA is monitored, as addressed in Article 3. This Addendum does not imply that all users, contracts, or data are governed by the GDPR.

Mandatory data protection law prevails over any inconsistent term in this Addendum.

## 2. Controller, Processor, and Customer Roles

Vira acts as a **controller** when determining purposes and means of account registration, authentication, onboarding, security, website analytics and advertising, support, and its own commercial administration.

Where a business customer determines purposes and means for personal data in its connected application and instructs Vira to process that data, the customer may be the **controller** and Vira its **processor**. If the customer acts on behalf of another controller, Vira's role may be sub-processor. Roles are assessed by activity, not solely by product tier.

Customer-directed processing requiring GDPR Article 28 terms is covered by the [DPA](dpa.en.md) **when adopted in a binding customer agreement**. Publishing a DPA page does not itself execute that agreement. Vira's own controller purposes remain subject to its Privacy Policy.

## 3. Categories of Data and Purposes

Depending on the enabled Service, information may include:
- Identity, email, phone, Google/GitHub account identifiers, and security session information.
- Company, country, role, intended use, project and workspace details.
- Authorized prompts, conversations, files, AI inputs/outputs, Behavior Packs, evaluations, and Teach data.
- Connected agent activity, tool-use details, trace identifiers, execution receipts, and operational logs.

Vira uses these categories to provide requested functionality, operate and secure accounts, administer support and contracts, and perform authorized analytics or advertising where legally permitted.

**Customer User Content is not used by Vira for general-purpose model training or general product/model improvement.** Authorized Observe/Teach processing is distinct from independent training of general-purpose models.

## 4. Legal Bases and Purpose Limitation

For Vira's controller processing, applicable GDPR Article 6 bases may include:

| Purpose | Potential legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Account registration, access, and core Service delivery | Contract necessity, Article 6(1)(b), where relevant |
| Essential security, fraud prevention, and limited operational interests | Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), following an appropriate balancing assessment |
| Mandatory accounting, legal obligations, or lawful orders | Legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c) |
| Non-essential analytics and advertising that require consent | Consent, Article 6(1)(a) |
| Optional marketing communications | Valid consent or another applicable lawful basis |

Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, with separate choices where needed. Withdrawal is available without retroactively invalidating processing performed lawfully before withdrawal.

Special categories under Article 9 and criminal-offence-related information under Article 10 are not required for ordinary account registration; when supplied through customer integrations, additional conditions and safeguards may be necessary. The customer remains responsible for appropriate notices and lawful grounds where it controls the processing.

## 7. Data Subject Rights

Where the GDPR applies, data subjects may have rights to:
1. Receive processing information and obtain access (Articles 12–15).
2. Have inaccurate personal data rectified (Article 16).
3. Request erasure where the legal conditions are met (Article 17).
4. Request restriction of processing (Article 18).
5. Obtain portability for qualifying data and processing (Article 20).
6. Object to qualifying processing, including direct marketing (Article 21).
7. Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis (Article 7).
8. Exercise applicable rights regarding solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Article 22).
9. Lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (Article 77).

Requests can be sent to **hello@tryvira.xyz**. We may verify identity and scope. Requests falling within Vira's controller role are answered within the applicable timeframe, normally **one month** after receipt, subject to lawful extensions with notice. Where a business customer is the controller, we may refer a request to that customer and provide assistance under the DPA.

## 8. Automated Decisions and AI Behavior

Vira enables customers to specify and evaluate AI behavior; its monitoring and Teach functionality may generate proposed behavior changes. These proposals do not themselves authorize production release.

The use of AI in a service does not, without further analysis, mean every person is subject to a decision covered by GDPR Article 22. Where a customer uses connected AI systems to make legally or similarly significant decisions, that customer must evaluate the applicable requirements and safeguards. Vira shall provide assistance where it is required as the processor under the accepted agreement.

## 9. International Transfers

Primary customer data storage is configured in the **European Union**, but transfers or remote access may occur through third-party inference providers, advertising, authentication, analytics, support, or customer-selected services.

For personal data subject to Chapter V of the GDPR, transfers to a third country require an appropriate legal basis such as an adequacy decision applicable to the particular recipient, Standard Contractual Clauses **actually entered into by the appropriate parties**, or another permitted transfer mechanism.

The [EU Transfer SCC Framework](eu-transfer-scc.md) explains the distinction between the transfer clauses under Commission Decision **(EU) 2021/914** and controller-processor standard clauses under Decision **(EU) 2021/915**. Vira does not claim that publishing either document automatically makes a transfer lawful.

A data exporter must also consider the destination's legal environment and, when needed, transfer-impact assessments and supplementary safeguards.

## 10. Sub-processors and Connected Providers

The [Provider Register](subprocessors.en.md) distinguishes infrastructure services, website analytics/advertising recipients, OAuth providers, and optional AI services. Not every listed vendor processes every customer's data.

For customer-directed processing, the relevant DPA governs authorization of sub-processors, due diligence, downstream protections, and appropriate change notices. The identity and location of external AI providers can depend on the accepted service configuration or the customer's chosen credentials.

## 11. Security, Breaches, and Data Protection Impact Assessments

Vira uses risk-based authentication, scoped access, credential protection, monitoring, and relevant security measures. The source availability of a particular protection does not independently prove complete deployment coverage or external security certification.

Where Vira is a controller, it assesses and carries out applicable security, personal-data-breach, and impact-assessment obligations, including regulator and data subject notices when the conditions of GDPR Articles 33 and 34 are met. Where Vira is a processor, it notifies the controller **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a qualifying personal data breach and assists under the agreed DPA.

A controller notification to a supervisory authority may be required within **72 hours of awareness where feasible**, depending on the legal criteria and the processing role.

Retention depends on processing purposes, account/workspace lifecycle, relevant customer instructions, provider configurations, and applicable law. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md) for data-category criteria.

Following account deletion, information subject to deletion is deleted within **30 days**, except for legally justified retention. Eligible Observe and Teach data may be requested for deletion separately; provider-side records and legally required evidence may have distinct obligations.
