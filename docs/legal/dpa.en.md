**Version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

## 1. Parties and Applicability

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") applies when a business customer (the "Customer") engages **Esad Türkel**, an independent individual operator of Vira in Istanbul, Türkiye ("Vira"), to process personal data on the Customer's behalf through Vira-operated Services.

This DPA becomes part of the parties' agreement only when incorporated into an accepted commercial order, a signed agreement, or another legally valid written acceptance expressly identifying this DPA. Merely reading or accessing this document does not establish a data processing agreement.

The Customer is the **Controller**, or a Processor acting under an authorized Controller, and Vira is the **Processor** (or sub-processor, as applicable) solely for the Customer Personal Data described below. For Vira's independently determined account, marketing, security, and website processing, Vira may be a separate controller; see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).

In case of conflict regarding processing of Customer Personal Data, this DPA prevails over the [Terms of Service](terms.en.md) to the extent necessary to meet mandatory data protection requirements. Other commercial terms remain governed by the accepted agreement.

## 2. Definitions

"Customer Personal Data" means personal data submitted to Vira, or collected through Customer-authorized integrations, that Vira processes on the Customer's documented instructions.

"Applicable Data Protection Law" includes GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK to the extent each applies to the specific processing.

"Sub-processor" means another processor engaged by Vira to process Customer Personal Data on Vira's behalf. An independent third-party controller is not automatically a Sub-processor.

"Security Incident" means a confirmed breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Customer Personal Data.

## 3. Processing Instructions and Responsibilities

Vira shall process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including instructions embodied in the accepted agreement, configured account permissions, supported product functionality, and authorized API requests, unless legally required otherwise. If legally required to process data outside instructions, Vira shall inform the Customer beforehand unless prohibited by applicable law.

Vira shall promptly inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates applicable data protection law, without assuming the Customer's overall compliance duties.

The Customer is responsible for identifying an appropriate legal basis, giving legally required notices, handling individual rights, limiting collection to necessary information, and obtaining authority for all conversations, files, connected systems, and end-user information supplied to Vira.

The Customer shall not instruct Vira to carry out processing that violates law or the authorization scope of the Services.

## 4. Nature, Purpose, and Duration

The processing details are set out in **Annex A** and may be narrowed or supplemented through the Customer's accepted service order and enabled configuration.

Processing takes place for the duration of the accepted Services and such post-termination period as is required to return, delete, or lawfully retain data. The nature and purpose include secure ingestion, storage, retrieval, organization, transmission, evaluation, Observe/Teach processing, behavior management, runtime evidence, and related support as instructed.

## 5. Confidentiality and Personnel

Vira shall restrict access to Customer Personal Data to persons who need it for authorized delivery, security, or support and are subject to enforceable confidentiality obligations or an appropriate statutory duty. Access shall be reviewed and revoked when no longer necessary.

Vira shall not disclose Customer Personal Data for unrelated purposes or use it for general-purpose model training or general product improvement.

## 6. Security Measures

Vira shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures considering the nature and risks of the processing, including access control, scoped permissions, credential protection, relevant transport security, operational monitoring, and incident handling. The measures described in **Annex B** define the applicable categories; deployment-specific measures may be detailed in an agreed security schedule.

No blanket promise of independent certification, encryption of every data field, or identical security architecture across all connected third-party systems is made.

The Customer remains responsible for securing its own endpoints, credentials, agent tools, and chosen external integrations.

## 7. Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the processing and available information, Vira shall reasonably assist the Customer in responding to lawful access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and similar requests as required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

Where a data subject submits a request directly to Vira concerning Customer-controlled data, Vira may refer the person to the Customer and shall not independently decide the merits of the request unless law requires otherwise.

Assistance may require verification of identity, account scope, and Customer authorization.

## 8. Security Incidents

Vira shall inform the Customer **without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident** affecting Customer Personal Data and provide information reasonably available about its nature, affected data, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed.

Where complete details are not initially available, information may be provided in stages. Vira shall cooperate reasonably with the Customer to investigate, contain, and remediate the incident and support legally required notifications.

The Customer remains responsible for determining and making its own notifications to authorities and individuals, unless the parties agree otherwise or the law directly requires action by Vira.

## 9. Sub-processors

By accepting this DPA, the Customer gives **general written authorization** for Vira to use Sub-processors identified for the applicable Services in the [Third-Party Services and Sub-processors Register](subprocessors.en.md), to the extent they act as processors of Customer Personal Data.

Vira shall inform the Customer of intended material additions or replacements of relevant Sub-processors in advance where legally required, through a suitable written service or contract communication, and provide a reasonable opportunity to object on documented data protection grounds.

Vira shall impose data protection obligations on relevant Sub-processors substantially equivalent to those in this DPA to the extent required by applicable law and shall remain responsible for their performance as required by that law.

The public register distinguishes Sub-processors from independent controllers and conditional AI providers. An unapproved or unconfigured provider is not automatically authorized to receive Customer Personal Data merely because a product integration could technically support it.

If an objection cannot reasonably be resolved, the Customer may discontinue the affected processing under the parties' contract and applicable law.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Vira's primary customer data storage is configured in the **European Union**, but remote access, independent third-party services, and customer-selected AI providers can involve processing elsewhere.

This DPA **does not itself constitute** the European Commission's international transfer Standard Contractual Clauses, Türkiye's statutory standard contract, an adequacy decision, or another transfer authorization.

Where a transfer requires additional safeguards, the parties shall put an appropriate mechanism in place before that transfer, such as applicable Standard Contractual Clauses with required annexes and supplementary measures, where legally suitable. The Customer's instructions do not override mandatory transfer rules.

## 11. Data Return, Deletion, and Retention

At the end of the Services, Vira shall, at the Customer's choice where applicable and technically supported, return or delete Customer Personal Data as instructed, unless a legal obligation requires retention.

Eligible account data subject to deletion is covered by Vira's **30-day post-account-deletion** process, subject to legally justified exceptions. Other customer-requested erasure may depend on the kind of data, accessible export/deletion tools, backups, and verified instructions. Vira shall not retain personal data solely because a storage quota remains unused.

Retention of legally required records shall be limited and protected; backup recovery cycles and provider-side records may be subject to specific arrangements that must respect applicable deletion duties.

## 12. Information, Demonstration of Compliance, and Audits

Vira shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law and this DPA, subject to confidentiality and security safeguards.

Where required by GDPR Article 28(3)(h), Vira shall allow and contribute to appropriate audits or inspections conducted by the Customer or its authorized independent auditor. The parties shall coordinate scope, timing, security restrictions, confidential information, and reasonable operational impact. Reports or third-party audit materials may be used where suitable but do not remove non-waivable statutory audit rights.

## 13. Assistance with Compliance

Taking into account the processing and information available to Vira, Vira shall reasonably assist the Customer with applicable security assessments, data protection impact assessments, and required regulatory consultations, as required by law.

Vira and Customer shall cooperate to document the processing and allocate responsibility for information held solely by the Customer or its external AI providers.

## 14. Liability, Term, and Legal Rights

Liability is governed by the accepted commercial agreement and mandatory law. No provision of this DPA reduces rights or duties that cannot lawfully be excluded.

This DPA remains effective while Vira processes Customer Personal Data for the Customer under an agreement adopting it. Changes to substantive processing obligations require a lawful agreement or an update handled under the accepted contract and mandatory law.

## Annex B — Security Measure Categories

Security controls are risk-based and may include:
1. Authentication and scoped organization/project/environment access checks.
2. Credential hashing, credential revocation, and separate protection for applicable secret values.
3. HTTPS/TLS configuration on supported public service endpoints and security checks on effectful integrations.
4. Limited operational access, audit/event records, monitoring, and secret redaction in relevant telemetry.
5. Controls for separating authorized data flows and preventing cross-tenant access.
6. Backup, recovery, incident response, data minimization, and deletion processes appropriate to each deployment.

The implementation and coverage of particular controls depend on the live service configuration; this Annex does not certify that every optional control is universally deployed.
